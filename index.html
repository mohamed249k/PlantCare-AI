How it works

1
Upload a clear leaf photo.

Use one clear, well-lit image of a leaf from tomato, potato, or bell pepper.

2
The trained MobileNetV2 ranks 15 visual classes.

Evaluates against the validation cutoff (0.593255) for selective accuracy.

3
Review cautious, source-linked next steps.

Access scientific university extension guidance and sanitary management.

Important limitation

This is an AI-assisted visual suggestion, not a confirmed agricultural diagnosis. Symptoms can overlap and a single image may not show the full plant. The displayed score is an uncalibrated model confidence score, not a probability.
